1. What we collect

WhoInformation
EveryoneName, phone number, email address, password (stored by our login provider, not readable by us), device and app information, push-notification address, and any error reports the app sends.
CustomersProfile photo (optional), ID number (where you give it), pickup and drop-off addresses, trip details and prices, ratings, messages and in-app call set-up data, and your live location while a trip is active.
DriversSouth African ID number, photo of your driver's licence, PrDP and vehicle licence disc, selfie and truck photo, vehicle details (make, type, number plate, VIN, engine number, payload), document expiry dates, results of automated document scans, subscription and payment records (including proof of payment you upload), ratings, and your live location while you are online or on a job.

We do not collect bank account details from drivers. Customers pay drivers directly, in cash, outside the app.

2. Why we use it

  • To create your account and match customers with drivers.
  • To check that drivers are who they say they are and hold valid documents, before they can take jobs. Automated tools read the documents to help our staff, but a person reviews and decides every approval.
  • To run trips: show drivers where to go, show customers where their driver is, let you message or call each other, and record proof of delivery and ratings.
  • To collect the driver subscription and confirm payments.
  • To keep the platform safe, handle complaints and disputes, prevent fraud, and meet legal duties.
  • To fix bugs and improve the apps.

We only use your information for these purposes, or for a closely related purpose, or where the law allows or requires it. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Who sees your information

Other users

  • Customers see the driver's name, photo, truck type, number plate, rating and live location during a trip.
  • Drivers see the trip details and the customer's name and the details needed to do the job. Drivers cannot see a customer's ID number.
  • Customers can share a trip-tracking link. Anyone with that link can see the driver's name, truck, number plate and live position until the trip ends. Share it only with people you trust.
  • A driver's ID number, licence, PrDP and disc photos, and scan results are kept in a restricted record that other customers and drivers cannot read.

Our team

Authorised TruckConnect administrators see account details and driver documents to approve drivers, support users and resolve disputes.

Service providers (operators)

We use these providers to run the service. They process information for us and may only use it for that purpose:

  • Google (Firebase login, database and push notifications; Google Maps and Places).
  • Cloudflare (our server functions) and GitHub (website hosting).
  • Mindee (reads driver document images to extract expiry dates and details).
  • Open road-routing services based on OpenStreetMap data (they receive the start and end coordinates of a route to calculate it).
  • Payment providers, where card billing is offered.

Some of these providers store information outside South Africa. We only use providers that give a level of protection comparable to POPIA, and we rely on your consent and our contracts with them for those transfers.

Legal

We may disclose information to the police, courts or regulators where the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

4. Location

The apps use your phone's location, only while you use the app for a trip or while you are online as a driver. Customers' location is used to set pickup points and track a trip. Drivers' location is shared with the customer during an active job, and with us so we can match nearby jobs. You can switch location off in your phone settings, but the trip and online features will not work properly without it.

5. How long we keep it, and security

We keep your information while your account is active and for as long afterwards as we need it for disputes, safety, accounting or the law. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your information within 30 days, except records we must keep by law (for example payment records) or need to deal with an open dispute or safety report.

We protect information with access controls, so each person can only read what they need, encrypted connections, and extra encryption on some sensitive fields. No system is perfectly secure. If there is a breach that affects you, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

6. Your rights

You may ask us to tell you what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it for a purpose. You may also withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing may mean we can no longer provide the service. Email the Information Officer above. We may need to confirm who you are first.

If you are unhappy with how we handled your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa): JD House, 27 Stiemens Street, Braamfontein, Johannesburg, 2001 · complaints.IR@justice.gov.za · www.inforegulator.org.za.

7. Children

TruckConnect is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Storage on your device

The apps save small amounts of data on your phone (for example your login, theme choice and saved routes) so they work faster and offline. We do not use advertising trackers.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. If the change is important, we will tell you in the app. The date at the top shows the latest version.
